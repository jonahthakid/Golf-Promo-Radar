Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

SKRATCH RADAR ("Radar," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Skratch.Golf, a property of Pro Shop Holdings, Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

Information collected automatically. When you visit Radar, we may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device type, browser type, and operating system
  • IP address (anonymized)
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, and time spent on the site
  • Referring URL

Information you provide. If you sign up for email alerts or create an account in the future, we will collect the information you provide, such as your email address.

Information we do not collect. Radar does not currently collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, address, or payment information. We do not sell any personal data.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve Radar
  • Understand how users interact with the site (e.g., which deals are most popular)
  • Attribute affiliate referrals to appropriate brand partners
  • Monitor site performance and troubleshoot issues

3. Affiliate Links and Third-Party Services

Radar contains affiliate links. When you click a link to a brand or retailer, you may be redirected through an affiliate tracking service before arriving at the destination site. This allows us to earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Impact.com - Affiliate link tracking and attribution. Impact may set cookies to track referrals.
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) - Site analytics. Google may set cookies (_ga, _gid) to distinguish users and sessions.

These third-party services may collect information about your browsing activity across other websites. We do not control how these services use data they collect.

4. Cookies

Radar uses a small number of cookies:

  • Session cookie - A functional cookie used for site operation (httpOnly, not used for tracking).
  • _ga, _gid - Set by Google Analytics to distinguish users and measure site traffic.
  • Affiliate cookies - Set by Impact.com when you click an affiliate link, used to attribute purchases.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

5. Data Retention

Aggregated, non-personal analytics data is retained for up to 14 months (the GA4 default). Click-level data stored on our servers is automatically deleted after 90 days. We do not maintain long-term records of individual user activity.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share anonymized, aggregated data with brand partners (e.g., "this deal received 500 clicks") for reporting purposes. No individual user data is shared.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking (via browser settings or a Do Not Track signal)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

Radar is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Brand and Content Removal Requests

If you are a brand, retailer, or rights holder and would like your promotions or listings removed from Radar, please contact us at radar@proshop.inc. We will process removal requests promptly, typically within 2 business days.

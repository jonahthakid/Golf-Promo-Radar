PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: February 23, 2026

WHO WE ARE

RADAR is operated by Skratch Golf, a property of Pro Shop Holdings. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the RADAR golf deals platform ("Service").

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Usage Data: When you visit RADAR, we automatically collect standard web analytics data including pages viewed, time spent, referring URLs, browser type, device type, and approximate geographic location. This data is collected through Google Analytics and is used in aggregate to improve the Service.

Click Data: When you click on product links or deals, we log the brand name, destination URL, traffic source, and timestamp. This helps us understand which deals are most valuable to our users and optimize the Service.

What We Don't Collect: RADAR does not require account creation. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or any other personally identifiable information. There are no user accounts, no login, and no forms that collect personal data.

COOKIES AND TRACKING

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use RADAR. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or any third-party tracking beyond Google Analytics.

AFFILIATE LINKS

RADAR contains affiliate links to third-party retailers and brands. When you click these links, you may be redirected through affiliate tracking services (such as Impact Radius) before arriving at the merchant's website. These affiliate partners may set their own cookies on your device.

We earn commissions on qualifying purchases made through these links at no additional cost to you. The presence of affiliate links does not influence which deals are displayed — RADAR scans all brands equally regardless of affiliate status.

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use the data we collect to operate and improve the Service, understand which deals and brands are most popular, monitor the Service for technical issues, and generate aggregate analytics reports.

DATA SHARING

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Aggregate, anonymized usage data may be shared with Pro Shop Holdings affiliates (Skratch Golf, GolfWRX, Sugarloaf Social Club) for business reporting purposes.

DATA RETENTION

Click tracking data is retained on a rolling basis (most recent 10,000 clicks). Google Analytics data is retained per Google's standard retention policies. We do not maintain long-term databases of individual user activity.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

RADAR is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy can be directed to the Skratch Golf team at skratch.golf.
